Process

From first call to launch day.

Every project follows the same four stages. Pick your type of project, then follow the stages to see who does what.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We learn your goals, audience and constraints, then agree scope, timeline and budget.

    We do

    • Run the kick-off call
    • Review what exists
    • Agree scope, timeline and budget

    You do

    • Share goals and access
    • Join the kick-off
    • Approve the plan

    Milestone: Plan and proposal approved

  2. 02

    Design

    Structure, layouts and prototypes you can react to early.

    We do

    • Map the structure and flows
    • Wireframe and prototype
    • Design the visuals

    You do

    • Share brand assets and examples
    • Give feedback on designs
    • Approve the designs

    Milestone: Designs approved

  3. 03

    Build

    Development in stages, with regular check-ins and a working version to review.

    We do

    • Develop in stages
    • Load content and test across devices
    • Share working versions

    You do

    • Supply final content
    • Review working versions
    • Send feedback as one list

    Milestone: Build ready for review

  4. 04

    Launch

    We go live, hand over and stay on hand afterwards.

    We do

    • Run final checks and go live
    • Hand over and train your team
    • Support you after launch

    You do

    • Give final sign-off
    • Plan your announcement
    • Tell us how it is going

    Milestone: Live and supported

  1. 01

    Research

    We study your market, audience and competitors, and agree what the brand must say.

    We do

    • Run a brand questionnaire
    • Review market and competitors
    • Define the positioning

    You do

    • Answer the questionnaire
    • Share what you like and dislike
    • Approve the brief

    Milestone: Creative brief approved

  2. 02

    Concept

    Directions for the identity, shown as early sketches and mood boards.

    We do

    • Create mood boards
    • Explore logo and type
    • Present the directions

    You do

    • Choose a direction
    • Give honest reactions
    • Share any must-haves

    Milestone: Direction chosen

  3. 03

    Refine

    We develop the chosen direction and test it in real situations.

    We do

    • Develop colour and typography
    • Apply it to print, social and web
    • Adjust through feedback rounds

    You do

    • Review the applications
    • Consolidate feedback
    • Approve the identity

    Milestone: Identity approved

  4. 04

    Guidelines

    We package everything so your team can use it consistently.

    We do

    • Write the brand guidelines
    • Prepare master files and templates
    • Hand everything over

    You do

    • Review the guidelines
    • Share them with your team
    • Start using the brand

    Milestone: Brand kit delivered

  1. 01

    Brief

    Goals, audience, where the content will be used, and the look and feel.

    We do

    • Run the kick-off call
    • Write the creative treatment
    • Plan budget and schedule

    You do

    • Share goals and references
    • Confirm where it will be used
    • Approve the plan

    Milestone: Shoot plan approved

  2. 02

    Pre-production

    Storyboards, shot lists, locations and bookings, organised before the camera rolls.

    We do

    • Write storyboards and shot lists
    • Secure locations and permits
    • Book talent and crew

    You do

    • Approve the storyboards
    • Confirm locations and people
    • Provide products or props

    Milestone: Ready to shoot

  3. 03

    Shoot

    A crew on set, with you in the loop.

    We do

    • Capture photo and video
    • Review on set with you
    • Back everything up safely

    You do

    • Be available on the day
    • Give feedback on set
    • Approve key shots

    Milestone: Everything captured

  4. 04

    Post-production

    Editing, colour, sound and retouching, then delivery in the formats you need.

    We do

    • Edit, colour, sound and retouch
    • Share cuts for review
    • Deliver in every format needed

    You do

    • Review cuts and give feedback
    • Approve the final edit
    • Receive your files

    Milestone: Final files delivered

Before we start

What we need from you.

01

Clear goals

What you want the project to achieve, and how you will know it worked.

02

Content and assets

Text, images, logos and files you already have, or a plan to create them with us.

03

Access

Logins, accounts and files we may need, shared safely.

04

A decision-maker

One person who can give feedback and approvals so work keeps moving.

How we work

Simple, steady, open.

01

One team

Design, development and production sit together, so nothing is lost in hand-offs.

02

Feedback built in

You see work early and often, so changes are small and cheap.

03

Plain communication

Clear updates, clear next steps, no jargon.

Questions

Good to know.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. We agree a timeline with you during the first stage, before any work begins.

How do you price projects?

We scope each project with you, then send a proposal after the first stage so you know what to expect.

How many rounds of feedback do I get?

We agree the rounds of feedback in the proposal, and you will see work early so changes stay small.

Can you work with what I already have?

Yes. Share your current site, brand files or footage and we will build on them where it makes sense.
